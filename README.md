# README.md — ZenDXB Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

**ZenDXB** is a healthcare and medical diagnostics platform operated by Zen Medical Clinic DMCC in Dubai, UAE. It provides laboratory testing, preventive health screening, reproductive health tests, at-home sample collection and medical consultations.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting ZenDXB as a convenient, accessible and trustworthy healthcare provider. It targets individuals, couples and professionals seeking medical testing and health screening, with clearer pathways to explore services and understand the testing process.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Positioning and Service Discovery
- Strengthened the homepage messaging to communicate ZenDXB's broader healthcare and diagnostics offering.
- Organised services into clearer categories, including diagnostic testing, health screening and medical consultations.
- Improved pathways to help visitors identify relevant services.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage concentrated heavily on individual tests and prices without clearly explaining the complete healthcare offering or guiding new visitors.

### 2. Stronger Clinical Trust and Credibility
- Prioritised the presentation of clinical credibility and relevant laboratory information.
- Identified the need for clearer explanations of testing methodology, laboratory credentials and clinical oversight.
- Emphasised transparent privacy information and substantiated healthcare claims.

**Problem addressed:** Existing claims about testing accuracy and technology lacked sufficient supporting clinical context, potentially making it harder for customers to establish confidence.

### 3. Clearer Testing Journey and Customer Guidance
- Introduced a clearer structure for explaining the testing process.
- Prioritised preparation instructions, sample collection information and result timelines.
- Identified opportunities for test-specific FAQs and more accessible consultation support.

**Problem addressed:** The previous product catalogue did not sufficiently explain what customers should expect before, during and after purchasing a test.

## 3. Design Improvements

The homepage redesign focuses on improving the overall presentation and customer experience through:

- **Visual presentation:** Clearer healthcare-focused messaging and a more professional brand presentation.
- **Layout:** Better organisation of testing products, screening packages and consultation services.
- **User experience:** More intuitive service discovery and clearer pathways through the testing journey.
- **Consistency:** A more structured presentation of healthcare information and service categories.
- **Information hierarchy:** Greater emphasis on clinical credibility, essential service information and customer guidance.

## 5. Project Summary

The ZenDXB homepage redesign addresses three important areas: clearer healthcare positioning, stronger clinical trust and improved testing guidance.

The overall direction is to create a more informative and professional healthcare experience that helps visitors discover relevant tests, understand the diagnostic process and make more confident decisions about accessing healthcare services.

**Implementation note:** Laboratory accreditations, clinical credentials, testing accuracy claims and regulatory information must be independently verified before publication. The advertising licence displayed on the existing website should not be presented as laboratory accreditation.
